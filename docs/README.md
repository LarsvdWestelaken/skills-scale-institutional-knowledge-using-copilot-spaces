# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This directory contains OctoAcme’s guidance for managing cross-functional projects from initial idea through delivery and learning. The process is grounded in customer value, iterative delivery, clear ownership, data-informed decisions, and psychological safety.

## Project lifecycle at a glance

1. **Initiate** — Validate the business need, define the goal and success measures, identify stakeholders, outline milestones and risks, and decide whether the work is ready for planning.
2. **Plan** — Turn approved work into a prioritized, estimated backlog with acceptance criteria. Agree on the Definition of Done, responsibilities, dependencies, risks, and release milestones.
3. **Execute and track** — Deliver in small increments, track work on a project board, and use reviews and regular team syncs to surface progress, blockers, and changing risks.
4. **Release** — Verify acceptance criteria, CI and security checks, prepare release notes and rollback plans, test in staging, deploy, and verify production.
5. **Close and improve** — Review the project, sprint, release, or incident; capture learnings and track a small number of owned, time-bound improvement actions.

Risk management and communication run throughout the lifecycle: teams maintain and review a risk register, share regular status updates, and escalate blockers through the PM and Product Lead, with sponsor escalation for business-impacting issues.

## Roles

- **Project Manager (PM):** Coordinates delivery, plans, schedules, risks, resources, and communications.
- **Product Manager (PdM):** Defines outcomes and success measures, prioritizes the backlog, and helps ensure the work delivers customer and business value.
- **Developers:** Design, implement, test, and document solutions, and contribute to planning and technical risk management.
- **QA/Testing:** Validates quality and acceptance criteria through appropriate testing.
- **Stakeholders:** Provide input, alignment, and approvals as needed.
